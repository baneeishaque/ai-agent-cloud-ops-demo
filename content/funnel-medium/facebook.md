> The funniest tech photo of the year is a developer carrying a half-open laptop through an
> airport — lid cracked open just enough to stop the agent from sleeping. I laughed, and then I
> realised I had done the same thing with a sugar packet in a café.
>
> A laptop is a bad home for an AI agent. It was built to sleep; the agent was built not to.
>
> So I moved my overnight price-and-inventory monitor to a small cloud instance I control from
> WhatsApp: instructions, alerts, and the 08:00 report all arrive in one chat, and every run
> saves a log file on the instance disk.
>
> The most useful thing I learned: make the agent report even when nothing happened. Otherwise
> "no message" could mean a quiet night — or a job that died at midnight.
>
> I wrote up the whole system, including the prompt line by line and the trade-offs:
