## Carousel (8 slides)

### Slide 1 — cover

> Your laptop is a bad home for an AI agent.
>
> (It was built to sleep. Your agent wasn't.)

### Slide 2 — the scene

> Developers carrying half-open laptops through airports — lid cracked just enough to keep the
> agent awake.
>
> The lid sensor became a job scheduler.

### Slide 3 — the failure

> A sleeping laptop fails three ways at once:
>
> lid close → sleep
> sleep → network drops
> network drop → background task paused

### Slide 4 — the timing

> For a team on Beijing time, peak shopping hours in Europe and the US happen overnight.
>
> That's when prices move — and when every laptop is closed.

### Slide 5 — the pattern

> Three planes, one instance:
>
> WhatsApp = control plane
> Cloud instance = compute plane
> Files on disk = evidence plane
>
> Closing the lid stops none of them.

### Slide 6 — the clause

> The most important line in the overnight prompt:
>
> "Send a report even if there are no issues."
>
> Without it, a quiet night and a dead job look identical. ✅

### Slide 7 — the guardrails

> "Monitor and recommend only: do not change prices, place purchase orders, or contact
> customers."
>
> That's policy, not a sandbox.
> Read-only credentials are the control. 🔒

> file on the instance.
>
